## Privacy Policy

Debtyy respects your privacy.

- Debtyy does not require an account and does **not collect** your name, email address or phone number.
- Your debts, people, amounts, notes, payments and attachments are stored **locally on your device**.
- iCloud sync is **optional** and fully controlled by the user. Data synced with iCloud is stored in the user’s private Apple iCloud account; the developer does not have access to it.
- **Anonymous usage statistics.** Debtyy uses TelemetryDeck to understand which features are used. It receives anonymous events (for example, that a debt was created or a screen was opened) together with the app version, device model, system version, language and the currency code of a debt. It never receives names, amounts, notes, attachments or contacts.
- **Purchases.** Payments are processed by Apple. To check that a purchase is active, Debtyy uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information. The developer does not receive your payment details.
- **No ads and no tracking.** No advertising identifier is used, and you are not tracked across other apps or websites.
- To delete your data, delete debts in the app or delete the app. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in your iCloud settings.
